# Goal

Give buggylaggy.com a favicon made from the new deadpan forced-smile logo, so browser tabs and bookmarks show it.

## Acceptance

- Browser tabs on buggylaggy.com show the tile favicon below (white face on tile blue), legible on light and dark tab
  bars; iOS home-screen bookmarks show the same face on a full tile-blue square.
- The favicon files are kept in `ErrantAnt/Assets/` beside the logo, and the site's copies are byte-identical.
- `npm run build` passes.

## Design

### Facts (verified 2026-10-27)

- The site has never had a favicon: no file in `public/` or in history, no `<link rel="icon">` in
  `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro`, and `https://buggylaggy.com/favicon.ico` (and `.svg`, `.png`,
  `apple-touch-icon.png`) return 404. Browsers show their default or a stale cached icon.
- The logo (archived task `20261027-pixel-logo`) is a 16×16 one-ink face, white `public/logo.svg` here, kept with its
  blue version in `ErrantAnt/Assets/`. Its smile spans columns 1–15.
- errantant.com ships `favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 px entries) and a 180 px `apple-touch-icon.png`, its mascot on a
  cobalt tile.

### The favicon (chosen 2026-10-27)

The white face on a pixel-rounded tile in the site's tile blue `#2f6ac3`, drop `#b0d0fd`. To clear the tile's edge at
16 px, the smile is one pixel shorter at each end and the drop sits one column left of the logo's.

```
..TTTTTTTTTTTT..
.TTTTTTTTTTTTTT.
TTTTTTTTTTTTLTTT
TTTTTTTTTTTLLLTT
TTTTTTTTTTTLLLTT
TTTTTTTTTTTTLTTT
TTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT
TTBBBBTTTBBBBBTT
TTTBBTTTTTTBBTTT
TTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT
TTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT
TTBTTTTTTTTTTBTT
TTTBTTTTTTTTBTTT
TTTTBBBBBBBBTTTT
.TTTTTTTTTTTTTT.
..TTTTTTTTTTTT..
```

`T` tile blue, `B` white, `L` drop, `.` transparent.

### Files

- `ErrantAnt/Assets/buggylaggy_favicon.ico`: 16, 32 and 48 px entries (1×, 2×, 3× cells), each a PNG-compressed RGBA
  image, as all current browsers read.
- `ErrantAnt/Assets/buggylaggy_apple_touch_icon_180.png`: 180×180 opaque, the whole square in tile blue (iOS rounds
  the corners itself and would fill transparent corners black), the grid at 11× (176 px) centred with a 2 px margin.
- Byte copies in this site's `public/favicon.ico` and `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, and in `BaseLayout.astro`
  `<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="16x16 32x32 48x48" />` and
  `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png" />`.
- The README's brand note names both copies' sources.
- Rejected: the bare logo face on transparency (thin at 16 px; one ink fades on one of the two tab themes).

## Scope

Favicon files, their copies in `ErrantAnt/Assets/`, `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro`, and the README's note on where the
brand files come from.

## Validation

- Files (2026-10-27): the ICO's directory decodes to three 32-bit entries, 16, 32 and 48 px, each a PNG of the same
  size (RGBA). The touch icon is 180×180 RGBA with every pixel opaque at the sampled points: corner `2f6ac3ff`, eye
  `ffffffff`, drop `b0d0fdff`. The 48 px entry, viewed, shows the white face and drop on the rounded tile; the touch
  icon, viewed, shows them on a full tile-blue square. `public/favicon.ico` and `public/apple-touch-icon.png` are
  byte-identical (`cmp`) to `ErrantAnt/Assets/buggylaggy_favicon.ico` and `buggylaggy_apple_touch_icon_180.png`.
- Site: `npm run build` passes; the built `dist/index.html` carries `<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico"
  sizes="16x16 32x32 48x48">` and `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">`, and
  `dist/favicon.ico` and `dist/apple-touch-icon.png` are byte-identical to `public/`. This replaced the planned
  dev-server 200 check: GitHub Pages serves `dist/` as files, so their presence there is the same evidence.
- Not covered: the icon in a real browser tab on the deployed site, which needs a push and a deploy.
